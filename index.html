HOW TO PLAY

Crew: finish your tasks (yellow markers) or vote out every impostor.

Impostor: kill crew, use vents, sabotage. Win when impostors ≥ crew.

Traitor (neutral): finish tasks OR get enough kills.

When you die your team decides your ghost: Wraith (impostor) haunts a crewmate to kill them unless a meeting is called · Spirit (crewmate) keeps doing tasks and fixing sabotages · Ghost (neutral) has no powers.

Controls: WASD/Arrows or touch-drag anywhere on the left of the screen. E use/task, Q kill, R report, F enter/exit vent, C ability, X sabotage, Esc pause.

Vents: impostors press F on a hatch to drop in. Inside, glowing arrows point at every linked vent — push that direction (or tap the arrow) to travel, F to climb out. Anyone standing in the room will see you vent.

Darkness: you only see your own room and the corridors leading out of it. Other rooms are dark and you can't see who's in them.

Systems: Admin table (Admin's Office), Cameras (Security), Vitals (Medbay), Emergency button (Living Room).

Start: after the role reveal you choose to enter through the Front Doors or the Back Doors — they open onto opposite wings of the mansion.

Score: +120 task, +200 kill, +150 correct vote, +80 report, +1000 win.
